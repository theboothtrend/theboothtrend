Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to THE BOOTH TREND ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our virtual photo booth application and website.

2. Camera Access and Face Tracking

Our virtual photo booth requires access to your device's camera to function. Please note:

  • Local Processing: All face tracking and rendering using MediaPipe technology happens entirely locally within your browser.
  • No Background Recording: We do not secretly record video or audio. The camera is only active while you are actively viewing the booth interface.

3. Image Data and Storage

When you capture a photo using our service, the image is generated locally on your device. We do not automatically upload, store, or host your images on our servers unless you explicitly opt-in to a specific branded campaign that requires server-side processing or live gallery hosting.

4. Information We Collect

We may collect standard web analytics data when you visit our site, including:

  • Device and browser information.
  • IP addresses and general location data.
  • Usage data (e.g., how many times a filter was used).

If you contact us via email or a contact form, we will collect your name, email address, and any contents of your message.
